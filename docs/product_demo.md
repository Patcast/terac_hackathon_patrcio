## What it is

A **fractional CFO as a service**. Small businesses can't afford a full-time CFO and don't
get one from their bookkeeper. Tamoa is an agent that reads the real ledger, answers
financial questions in plain language over iMessage, and escalates the calls that need
human judgment to actual finance experts.

It reads the books. It never writes to them.

## Who it's for

Small businesses and founders with real accounting data in Odoo, no finance hire, and
decisions to make about cash.

## The one-line pitch

> Text your CFO. It knows your books, and a real expert checks its work.

## Demo flow (draft)

1. Client texts the Tamoa number: *"Can I afford to hire someone in March?"*
2. Agent pulls live cash, burn, and AR aging from Odoo.
3. Claude reasons over it and replies with runway, the risk, and a recommendation —
   every number traceable to the ledger.
4. The call is material, so it's routed to a finance expert via Terac for review.
5. Expert rating comes back; the answer is updated and the improvement is recorded.
6. The engagement is invoiced via a Stripe link in the same thread. Card flips to Paid.
